Controls

Drag (or A/D, arrow keys) to aim; hold Shift for fine aim. Drag up/down to look. Q/E or the arrows change club. Space or SWING: first press starts the backswing, second sets power, third sets accuracy when the needle comes back to the white line. Too early slices right, too late hooks left. B sips, N takes a shot. Z zooms, C toggles ball cam, Tab shows the scorecard.
