Controls

Drag (or A/D, arrow keys) to aim; hold Shift for fine aim. Drag up/down to look. Q/E or the arrows change club. Space or SWING: first press starts the backswing, second sets power, third sets accuracy when the needle comes back to the white line. Too early slices right, too late hooks left. B sips, N takes a shot. Z zooms, C toggles ball cam, Tab shows the scorecard. After each shot you travel to your ball: W/S or ▲▼ to go, A/D or ◀▶ (or drag) to steer, G honks. Funnel a beer on the tee for a cart; otherwise you run. When you reach your ball, press ⛳ Play shot (or F); ⏭ Skip jumps there for a sip. In a cart, hit a friend to run them over; E (or 🚶 Get out / 🚗 Get in) leaves and boards your cart, which stays where you leave it. 🎯 (or X) drops an attack ball at your feet, five per hole: line up the crosshair, tap SWING to start the backswing and tap again to hit it (the needle sets the speed). It flies straight at whoever is under the crosshair; a hit knocks them down or ruins their swing. Attack balls never count as strokes. Putting: the putter's full stroke scales to the putt, and the blue ⛳ tick on the meter shows the power that reaches the hole on a flat putt; the dotted line shows how the putt starts to break.
